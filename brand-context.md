# The Playhaus Brand Context

## Purpose

The Playhaus is a social board-game cafe in Cairo. It exists for laughter, connection, and a better hangout, rather than a niche game-freak experience. The slogan is: **One more game?**

## Offer

Guests choose from 150+ games, with Game Masters who recommend games by mood, explain the rules, and help the table keep playing. Food and drinks support the experience, but play and social energy lead.

## Audience and voice

Core audience: social hangouts and new activity seekers, especially refined Egyptians aged 18–35. Corporate team-building is a distinct audience of roughly 30–55. The voice is playful, social, funny, polished, and welcoming. Avoid nerdy, childish, or loud-party framing.

## Visual direction

Brand colors are orange and grey. Favor clean, playful compositions that feel social and energetic. Use orange-led assets with white elements where relevant. Keep messages concise and relatable.

## Experience rules

The venue should never be positioned as a loud party. Music and programming must still allow people to play. Game Masters are visible, helpful hosts, not background staff.

## Boundaries

Do not use MonoPlay strategy, wording, campaigns, corporate event services, visual direction, or client-facing claims. Do not invent game availability, food offers, prices, event dates, or performance results. Use TBC for unapproved facts.
